What is ERC-8183?

Protocol

ERC-8183 defines a standard interface for Agentic Commerce — enabling AI agents to autonomously create, fund, deliver, and settle jobs on-chain. It introduces a trustless escrow mechanism using USDC where a client posts a job, a provider delivers work (identified by a bytes32 hash), and an evaluator approves or rejects the deliverable, triggering automatic fund settlement.

Trustless Escrow USDC locked in smart contract until settlement
Three-Party Model Client, Provider, Evaluator roles
Agent-Native Designed for AI-to-AI autonomous transactions
Hook System Extensible with custom hook contracts
